## About Me Section

**Title:** About me

**Paragraphs:**

I'm a full-stack software engineer with 5+ years across consulting, SaaS, and web agencies. I build products end-to-end — frontend, backend, infrastructure — and I care as much about the user experience as the architecture behind it.

I've built products from scratch where nobody had a clear spec and I had to piece together the requirements myself, and I've worked inside large, structured codebases where the real skill is setting conventions and patterns that keep everything stable as it grows. I like both, and switching between the two is what I am good at.

When I'm not working, I'm probably running, playing, tinkering with a side project or experimenting with something new. The lab never really closes.
