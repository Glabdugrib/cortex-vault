# AWS Region

- Cluster of data centers
- Most of AWS services are region-scoped

## How to choose an AWS Region
- **Compliance**: data governance and legal requirements
- **Proximity to users**: reduced latency
- **Available services**: new services and features aren't available in every Region
- **Pricing**: may vary from Region to Region

## Availabilty Zones
- One or more discrete data centers with redundant power, networking and connectivity
- Each Region has many (usually 3, min 3, max 6)
- Separate from each other to be isolated from disasters
- Connected with high bandwidth, ultra-low latency networking

	Example for Sydney Region ap-southeast:
	- ap-southeast-2a
	- ap-southeast-2b
	- ap-southeast-2c

## Points of Presence (Edge Locations)
- 400+ Points of Presence (400+ Edge Locations & 10+ Regional Caches)
- 90+ cities
- 40+ countries
- Content is delivered to users with low latency
